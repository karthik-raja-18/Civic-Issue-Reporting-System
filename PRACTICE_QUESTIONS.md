## Final Tips

After writing your answers:
1. **Practice out loud** - Say your answers to yourself or a friend
2. **Time yourself** - Keep answers to 2-3 minutes each
3. **Be specific** - Use actual file names, class names, and technologies
4. **Show enthusiasm** - Let your passion for the project come through
5. **Be honest** - If you don't know something, say so and explain how you'd learn

**Good luck! You've got this! 🚀**
